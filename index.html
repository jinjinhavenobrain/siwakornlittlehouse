HOUSE STUDIO / V5

A room creator for everyone.

Start here

Start from scratch: choose a room size and ceiling height, then create your empty room. All sizes are in metres.

Floors: in Build, pick one of eight stair or ladder styles and add an upper floor. Use Your floors to switch rooms, or View whole house to see them stacked. The upper floor has a real opening. Each floor keeps its own furniture and finishes; undo and JSON save the whole project.

Round tables: select the wooden round table and drag the tabletop diameter slider. Its top stays circular and its height is set separately.

Rooms: drag empty space to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan. Plan gives a true overhead view.

Build: select a wall, floor, door, window, bathroom or roof in the 3D scene or Build list. Choose marble, wood, matt tile and other floor finishes, then recolor without losing the pattern. Split a wall by clicking it or entering a distance; select a section and extend it as the attached bathroom. Draw wall adds a partition with two clicks in Plan. The Keep / hide controls set each wall to full, low or hidden.

Decorate: add an item, then drag it across the floor. Select it for exact size, rotation and colour. Use arrow keys to nudge, R to rotate, Delete to remove.

Simulate: click the floor or choose a destination. Blocky routes around walls and furniture, opens doors, and sits or sleeps on compatible items.

Save: autosave is local to this browser. Export JSON for a portable backup. Named layouts let you compare ideas.

Design assumptions

4.50 × 4.75 m main house; 1.50 × 3.00 m bathroom outside the rear-left wall; initial 4.50 × 1.00 m rear balcony. Sizes are modelled as outside-face dimensions, with 15 cm walls.

Wall top is 3.00 m and ridge 4.00 m above the finished floor. Floor is assumed 38 cm above the garden. Exact window, door and fixture positions are approximations.

Loft mattress top is 1.88 m: only about 1.12 m to a 3 m ceiling. Check the actual roof/ceiling, guardrails and ladder with your designer.

Bed labels are nominal widths (3, 3.5, 5, 6 feet). Inspect the model dimensions and compare the real frame before buying.

Concept visualization, not construction drawings. Collision and reachability checks are approximate. Garden and daylight are illustrative, not a surveyed plot or a solar study.

Your custom room

Start with an empty rectangular room. Edit width, length and ceiling height in Build. Draw wall adds interior dividers with two clicks in Plan. Select any item to move, resize or recolor it.

A project supports up to four floors. Stairs and ladders connect neighbouring floors, and upper floors have matching openings. The whole house view stacks floors at the chosen ceiling heights plus a 20 cm floor slab. Blocky explores one selected floor at a time.

This is a room-planning tool. The stair and ladder models are illustrative; their dimensions do not certify a construction-ready stair design.
